# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

## Stack

React, Vite, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, TanStack Query, Zustand, Wavesurfer.js, Tauri (desktop wrapper), Python FastAPI local backend.

## Users

Creators, developers, and power users who need high-quality voice cloning, speech generation, system-wide dictation, and voice agent interactions with complete privacy and ownership.

## Product Purpose

The open-source AI voice studio. Voicebox makes speech generation, voice cloning, audio transcription, and voice chat accessible entirely offline on user hardware. Success means reliable zero-latency or near-realtime local voice workflows without subscription fees or data leakage.

## Positioning

Full-stack voice I/O running 100% locally on personal hardware with zero cloud lock-in, zero subscription fees, and complete data sovereignty. Unlike proprietary cloud voice APIs, users own the models, audio pipelines, voices, and generated assets.

## Capabilities

- Voice cloning from short audio reference files.
- Text-to-speech across multiple local TTS engines (Kokoro, F5-TTS, Chatterbox, Piper, etc.).
- System-wide dictation and transcription.
- Voice agent conversations with custom voices.
- Local model management and hardware acceleration (CUDA, ROCm, Metal, CPU).

## Constraints

- 100% local execution required by default.
- Zero mandatory cloud dependencies or tracking.
- Audio playback and waveform visualization must remain responsive under heavy inference load.
